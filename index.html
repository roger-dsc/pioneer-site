Design

How it is built, and how it is run.

Nose
A long single arch that softens the pressure wave in tunnels, as on the E5 Shinkansen.
Body
Aluminium and composite, flush, with a covered underframe.
Pressure
Sealed, with active air control: the ears hardly notice a tunnel.
Traction
Silicon-carbide converters (N700S) and permanent-magnet motors (AGV): smaller, more efficient.
Brakes
Regenerative first; an eddy-current brake and carbon-ceramic discs, tested for the CR450, do the rest.
Suspension
Air springs, and active dampers that push against the body’s sway.
Tilting
Up to 1° into curves, as the N700 does to hold 270 km/h.
Signalling
ETCS Level 2 with automatic operation; the driver supervises.
Monitoring
Parts replaced on condition, not on a schedule or after they fail.
Cab
A cab at each end; the train is never turned.
Cabin
One class, warm light, a window at every row.
Quiet car
Car 6, set aside for silence.
Crew
Staff walk the train; help comes to the seat.
Luggage
In the car, within reach.
Station
One platform height; the doors stop at the same marks.
Lounge
At every stop, not only the ends.
Seat
Reclines within its own space.
Access
Level boarding; a wheelchair space in every car.
Energy
Braking energy goes back to the line.
Carbon
Rail 4.5 to 35 g of CO₂ per passenger-km; a car 167 g, a flight 229 g (UK government).
Construction
About 58 t of CO₂ per km a year to build and maintain, repaid within 10 to 30 years (UIC).
Land
One narrow double track, not a motorway.
Water
Viaducts keep streams and animal routes open.
Air
No exhaust at the platform.
Noise
A flush body and skirted bogies keep the noise at the line.
Maintenance
Condition monitoring and standard parts: more time in service.
Materials
Recyclable aluminium; composites built to come apart.
Capacity
3,300 seats an hour each way: eight cars, six trains an hour.
Cost
Few stops, one platform type: the money goes into the line.
Fare
By distance, published and fixed.
Development
Homes and offices around each station.
Extension
One stop at a time: new track, the same service.
Turnaround
15 minutes at each end; 15 trains, spares included, run the timetable.
City
The line ends inside the city, at Central.
Interchange
One level from the metro, not a station to cross.
Timetable
Timed to the metro: a wait of minutes.
Ticket
One ticket from the metro to the Frontier.
Access
Walking, cycling and buses at every stop, not a car park.
Streets
Viaducts through towns: the streets keep running underneath.
Station
One roof, open to the street on both sides.
